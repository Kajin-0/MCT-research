## A false impurity potential from two calculations of the same host

The native spinor cross-overlap measures whether the physical projected
orbitals coincide. For polar frames Q_old,Q_new and their Bloch states,

    S = Q_old* <psi_old|psi_new> Q_new.

**PROVEN:** matching trial labels and a unitary within each retained band
space do not establish S=I. Direct Hamiltonian subtraction requires a
common physical basis, or an explicit projection of both operators into
one fixed basis. This is an established requirement, discussed by
[Berlijn, Volja and Ku (2011)](https://link.aps.org/accepted/10.1103/PhysRevLett.106.077005).

**CALCULATED:** in these same-material controls, the Gamma physical-frame
overlap has minimum singular value 0.999999999995 and matrix difference
7.72 micro-eV. At X the minimum singular value is **0.109056** and the
same-label matrix difference is **6.30703 eV**. The maximum retained-band
energy change over the grid is only 32.275 meV.

The X spaces differ strongly, so that large matrix difference is invalid
as a physical substitution potential. Numerical error in the underlying
empty-state operator may also contribute. This control does not attribute
the complete difference to a basis rotation, and does not establish the
origin of the older tight-binding Born C pathology. It supplies a concrete
negative control for the extraction method that will produce the physical
Cd-in-HgTe potential.

## Exact host-eigenstate extraction and a finite-box pole trap

**PROVEN:** selecting actual orthonormal host edge spinors Phi gives an
invariant physical host projector, H0 Phi=Phi h0. Its host resolvent is
exactly R0=(z-h0)^(-1). From the impurity spectrum projected onto those
same physical spinors, R=Phi* (z-Himp)^(-1) Phi, one obtains

    T_P=(z-h0) R (z-h0)-(z-h0),
    K_P=(z-h0)-R^(-1).

The first is the ambient finite-volume projected T. The second is the
P-irreducible Feshbach reaction kernel. They are different objects. The
full impurity spectrum preserves nonlocal HSE exchange without a partial
operator export or separately relabeled Wannier frames. Window errors
still need certification; numerical eigenpair errors are additional.

**PROVEN finite-volume counterexample:** at an exact host band-edge pole,
the full finite-box T can tend to zero even while the reaction kernel
remains finite. Multiplying by the cell volume does not repair taking the
real-energy limit before the thermodynamic limit. A dilute forward shift
requires convergence of N K_P,N or the correctly ordered full-T limits,
including an explicit continuum prescription on the HgTe side.

The [host-invariant derivation](../../../docs/derivations/host_invariant_reaction_kernel.md)
states the locality/nonresonance assumptions, the complete Q scattering
and the spectral-window bounds. **CALCULATED verification:** ambient dense
T, exact Q-block inversion and the finite-box on-shell pitfall agree with
the new implementation. The companion DFT suite now passes **67 tests,
1 deselected**, at commit **152e801**. No physical paired SCF or endpoint
impurity value has been computed by this method.

**HYPOTHESIS:** this is a more direct route to the first endpoint mass

## Continuum prescription and a sharper crossing definition

**PROVEN:** taking the Hermitian part after retarded impurity resummation
is generally different from resumming the Hermitian/principal-value host
resolvent. Writing g=h-i gamma and K_h=T[h], the exact ordered relation is
T[g]=(I+i K_h gamma)^(-1)K_h. K_h is a support-space reactance, distinct
from the host-edge P-irreducible K_P. Off-continuum equality is retained.

**PROVEN correction to the crossing interpretation:** F_s=0 and Mfrak=0
characterize a shared pole, including a shared complex pole. Equal real
parts of two broadened sector poles do not require equal linewidths and
therefore do not imply a zero complex mass. The prospective mass-route
prediction must name the real-denominator, real pole-energy or spectral-peak
observable and retain the absorptive branch information.

**CALCULATED mathematical audit:** 21 records on 14^3, 22^3 and 30^3
midpoint grids verify the exact ordered conversion to 5.64e-16 maximum
relative error. CdTe real mid-gap gives the expected equality. At mesh
30^3 and eta=50 meV the archived HgTe model shows an operator difference
59.7839 meV but a mass projection difference only -1.31675 meV. This
model remains rejected as a physical predictor; no alternate x_c or
self-consistent static-repair claim is generated.
