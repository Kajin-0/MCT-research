## Source/access and lineage limits

The direct Nature/PMC URLs initially failed; the publisher's cookie-error URL
and authorPDF worked. Eq2 was inspected in the authorPDF, including its Landau
index definitions; no paper download or tool installation was needed. Orlita
HTML includes its supplement. Wei's authorPDF and Krishtopenko's fullHTML are
primary texts. Malcolm–Nicol fullHTML adds an independent current/line-shape
check. The earlier Alowa2025 comparison remains preview-only; this phase
makes no full-text claim about its contents and no novelty claim based on it.

Krishtopenko2022 TableI uses its Ref8; the existing repository input-lineage
audit traces that model's gap to Laurenti. Such values are barred as microscopic
inputs here. Far-infrared results are independent experimental checks of Kane
physics but have specimen/observation assumptions. We did not extract any
measured gap, composition, velocity, linewidth or critical concentration to set
our model coefficients. All numerical tests are explicitly synthetic.

R02's homogeneous8-band phase/symmetry implementation was read and its Gamma8/7
projectors independently reproduced. R05's full-Kane/optical warning, matched
incoherent-mixture null, absence of qualified covariance data and terminated
HgCdTe activation remain unchanged. This package belongs to R07, not a restart
or reclassification of R02/R05.
