This ONE multi-field response object allows a velocity-independent Q sign and

## Quantitative precision before acquisition

Do not prescribe meV accuracy without the actual line/window margin. Required
precision follows from D_hat>epsilon_D and |Q_hat|>epsilon_Q; a useful
third-field rejection needs discrepancy larger than its independently
propagated center, field and model errors. If the desired later composition
resolution is delta_x, a physical adapter must deliver
epsilon_opt+epsilon_model<=s delta_x with independent s. All such errors must
be prospective, not selected from the observed discrepancy. No material s,
Q, linewidth or root is numerically supplied here.

The simple equal-weight mixture produces bias proportional to mass variance
times(1/sqrt(B1)+1/sqrt(B2)); the actual-current example gives a larger bias
in the tested synthetic model. These formulas guide discriminating fields/
errors but are not measured HgCdTe variance estimates. No experimental numeric
material coefficient was used to tune them.
