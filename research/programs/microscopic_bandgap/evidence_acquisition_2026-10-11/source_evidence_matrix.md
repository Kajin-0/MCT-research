# Primary source–evidence matrix

Audit date: 2026-10-11. This matrix accompanies the [decision memo](decision_memo.md).
**No row supplies qualified physical D+B+S+U.** D means a common support-free
kernel domain with root-existence margins; B an interior mass-sign bracket on
that domain; S an all-pairs lower composition modulus; U uniform physical
branch residuals. “Not supplied” refers to certification of that target, not
absence of the reported bands, self-energy or experiment.

[sources.json](sources.json) records titles, DOI links, inspected locations,
access levels, primary-copy SHA-256 values and inherited audit paths. A fresh
abstract audit is explicitly weaker than a full-text audit. The source summaries
below are paraphrases; no publisher PDF, full extracted text or digitized curve
is distributed. Source numbers and model crossovers remain attributed results,
not new predictions or coefficients for R07.

## Coverage and residual access limits

Searches covered static HSE/mBJ/DD hybrid alloy Γ states; unfolded weights;
empirical TB/CPA; Kane/SCBA Green functions and random-mass correlations;
GW/QSGW and SOC; historical and modern magneto-optics; ARPES inversion;
zero-point shifts; support/error/modulus certification; and current 2025–2026
material work. The exact query families and access outcomes are recorded in
[sources.json](sources.json). Mathematical source chains remain in the earlier
[analytical literature audit](../analytical_first_2026-10-11/literature.md) and
[threshold literature audit](../threshold_causal_2026-10-11/literature.md).

Full-text failures P01/P08/P09/P18 and inherited rather than freshly recovered
historical copies are explicit limits. This is a bounded primary-source audit,
not proof that no unlocated result exists. These limits do not justify importing
unknown parameters. Even granting the retrieved model spectra as exact outputs
of their stated approximations, the inspected sources do not certify physical
D/S/U or a corresponding B. The decisive conclusion is therefore missing
qualified pre-existing input, rather than absence of all alloy evidence.
