## Complete uncertainty checklist

For all candidate rows the accessible bundle lacks at least one of each
required chain: specimen identity → independently calibrated assay → spatial
sampling over the optical footprint; acquired polarization → leakage/orientation
calibration; raw signal/reference/dark → photon/field/temperature calibration →
repeats and correlated systematic errors. Strain, thickness/grading, carrier
filling, phonon/substrate opacity and model discrepancy also need bounded
specimen-specific treatment. A published fit standard deviation is not a bound
on this joint chain. Unknowns are not repaired by importing Hansen/Laurenti.

The inherited source registry's earlier Guldner x=0.150 “exact caption” shorthand
does not override the later direct Part II audit's **approximate** composition
and gap qualifiers. Its crossover uncertainty is not an individual assay error.
No older data record is edited by this intake task.

Local CSV/JSON records are source transcriptions, calculated outputs or fitted
gap summaries. No raw instrumental archive was found in the inspected
experimental/evidence/Hansen/literature corpora or paper library manifest.

currently mounted; a PDF attachment itself would not satisfy raw-data intake.
