# Domain D: physical plausibility and a weaker experimental bracket

Stage-0 assessment, 2026-10-11. No material kernel or spectral fit is calculated.
The [existing threshold theorems](../threshold_causal_2026-10-11/derivation.md)
remain conditional. D concerns the support of both **eliminated weighted kernel
measures**, with common-root existence margins; it is not a statement that
optical absorption is weak or the signed Gamma6–Gamma8 separation is nonzero.

## Why a common support-free interval is doubtful on the inverted side

**Primary physical premise:** unstrained inverted HgTe/HgCdTe has a touching
Gamma8 sector and a heavy-hole dispersion, not a semiconductor excitation gap
of magnitude |E6−E8|. The
[Orlita author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/1310.0969) discusses this
distinction and the downward quadratic heavy-hole curvature. Its ideal flat-band
approximation must not be treated as an exact absence of states below E8.

**PROVEN UNDER MODEL ASSUMPTIONS, plausibility witness:** set E8=0, E6=Eg<0,
and Ehh(k)=−a|k|² with a>0. The bare common energy Eg/2 lies in the heavy-hole
continuum provided this dispersion extends to that energy. For a projected
coupling W nonzero on its energy shell,

```text
rho_W(E) = integral d^3k |W(k)|^2 delta(E+a|k|^2)/(2pi)^3 > 0,
-Im K^R(E) = pi rho_W(E) > 0.
```

For constant W, rho_W is proportional to sqrt(−E). Thus this channel has no
support-free real neighbourhood of the candidate common energy. This is a
conditional continuum mechanism, not a reconstruction of the exact alloy's
augmented-space measure or proof that its physical Ebar stays at Eg/2.
Finite-q substitution and the already-derived directional/SOC channel allow
such scattering; symmetry permission does not prove a nonzero material W.

**HYPOTHESIS / adverse plausibility assessment:** with generic nonzero alloy
coupling on the inverted side, D is unlikely to hold across the proposed
two-sided interval without an additional decoupling/separation argument.
Neither bulk semimetallicity alone nor full operator support proves the support
of a particular projected measure. At a pure translationally invariant endpoint,
the retained exact Gamma states can decouple and have zero disorder kernel
despite other k-state continua. A weighted-selection zero, an adequately bounded
strain gap, or another independently demonstrated separation could change the
assessment; none is supplied here. Strain also changes the intended quartet
observable and requires an adapter, rather than silently opening D.

On the normal side a clean gap can make separation plausible, but disorder
tails and reference/projector choice still require qualification. At closure,
a finite one-sided threshold value is allowed by the theorem; an interval
through spectral support is not. The established continuum counterexample
falsifies replacing D by retarded causality alone. Resolution-limited optical
silence and a finite eta cannot certify exact zero weighted support.

## What can be bracketed without D?

**PROVEN UNDER ASSUMPTIONS, ordinary intermediate-value statement:** let
Gop(x;T,s) be a continuously defined, sign-sensitive experimental ordering
observable for one fixed low temperature T and declared growth/strain/carrier
state family s. Suppose a justified observation operator and complete error
envelopes establish Gop(xL)<0 and Gop(xR)>0, with independent assay intervals
[xL−,xL+] and [xR−,xR+] ordered by xL+<xR−. Then at least one zero of Gop
lies in [xL−,xR+]. This needs neither a support-free Feshbach domain nor a
composition derivative bound. It does need continuity between the actual
specimens; two unrelated strains/carrier states do not establish a single path.

With the pilot's design assays 0.150±0.002 and 0.185±0.002 this would be the
operational existence interval [0.148,0.187]. These are design numbers, not
accepted assays or a newly inferred bracket. No midpoint or stage-1 fit is run.

The statement certifies **an operational finite-temperature reversal**, not
mu on the common trajectory. Finite linewidth and energy-dependent kernels
separate peak, real-inverse and complex-pole crossings. T17's denominator-sign
transfer and T16's max(epsilon6,epsilon8)/m error certificate still require D
and their other gates. Uniqueness, physical 0-K x_c, a mobility edge or a
topological boundary do not follow. The raw/metrology failure prevents a new
operational certificate now; published model-assisted signs retain their
original evidential scope.
