## Domain D and the achievable weaker claim

**D remains OPEN, with an adverse physical plausibility assessment on the
inverted side.** The negative signed Gamma6–Gamma8 gap is not a forbidden-energy
interval. A heavy-hole continuum with nonzero disorder coupling can cover a
candidate common energy. Decoupling and weighted support, rather than an optical
null or nominal gap, would have to justify D. The
[domain note](stage0_domain_and_sign_bracket.md) preserves the conditional
theorems and states exactly why optical data do not certify D.

Without D, a successful later reconstruction could establish robust opposite
**operational finite-temperature ordering signs**, and, with continuity for the
same specimen-state family, existence of at least one ordering reversal between
the independently assayed specimens. It would not establish the common-mode
mass bracket of T17, uniqueness, T16's root bound, physical 0-K x_c or a
topological boundary. This weaker bracket is not newly certified by Stage 0.
