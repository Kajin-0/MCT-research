specimens must fall within ±0.002 of their targets with independently justified
composition half-width ≤0.002 including spatial/calibration effects; raw

uncertainty is inferred from the paper's reported crossover uncertainty.

If retention cannot be confirmed, the raw/assay linkage is missing, or either
specimen fails those gates, retain NO-GO. Do not automatically replace this
