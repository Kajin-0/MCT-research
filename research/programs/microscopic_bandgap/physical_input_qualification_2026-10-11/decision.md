## One design, with a measurable but limited gain

The [design](minimum_acquisition.md) fixes a numerical probe W=0.5 eV,
eta=0.025 eV and its native branch-midpoint reference. Eta is analytical
resolution. It asks whether matched static HSE substitution has P-to-Q coupling
and resolvable mass/common response in a qualified spectral-host frame.
Native moments suffice for the bounded ordering functional; a full arbitrary
Wannier vertex is unnecessary. Leakage additionally needs inverse conditioning.

At p=0.05, a <=0.5-meV sector tail allocation admits sigma<=3.887e-5.
An unstructured global operator-norm bound would demand approximately
0.141 micro-eV for the other half-budget: that precision requirement is
**rejected**, not assigned to a future convergence queue. Seek independent
observable residual bounds instead. SCF total-energy convergence is not one.

If the pilot passes all conditional gates, its numerical finite-cell ordering
error becomes <=2 meV at one predeclared probe. The physical x_c uncertainty
has **no proven reduction** because model, image, alloy-law and sharp-observable
inputs are missing. A positive leakage trace can reject a closed-P model;
a null result below budget is inconclusive. No prescribed A/C value is a target.

Full-SCF runtime and peak RAM remain unmeasured. The prior f120 dry estimate
is about 21.3 GiB; the f360 pair estimate exceeds the recorded machine budget.
The design's finite planning cap is not authorization or a convergence promise.
No deck, native calculation, queue, paid action, outreach or supervisor operation
was created. Future progress needs an independently acquired packet or explicit
authorization for a separately owned, resource-reviewed acquisition. No request
for blanket permission is made here.
