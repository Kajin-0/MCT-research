# Admission decision: what the new evidence can change

**ADVANCE owned-family hardening; NO native execution authority issued.**
The previous lack-of-an-accessible-control observation is superseded by actual
UID1001 user-manager/kernel evidence. Add verified cgroup controls to the owned
family backend and retain the existing monitor/lease/checkpoint predicates.
Do not replace summed RSS with memory.current or treat limit configuration as
a reserved-memory/completion certificate.

The implication “our cgroup has a hard cap => no foreign MPI exists” is false.
Counterexample: two hosts present identical owned group limits, invocation,
rank receipts, worker outputs and cleanup. One also has a foreign MPI-loaded
process outside our group. It neither changes our membership nor the supplied
receipt. Systemd and cgroup data about our unit cannot distinguish those hosts.
The old non-identifiability result and unreadable-inventory HARD_BLOCKED remain.
No new inventory search, namespace hiding, permission change, sudo, Docker,
broker installation or blanket approval is attempted.

**Non-weakening protocol amendment supported by current evidence:** require
verified user-manager invocation, kernel memory/swap/task limits, group cleanup,
fd escrow and durable ownership marker **in addition to** original native
input/backend/profile/common-wave/readback/resource/host-wide gates. Native
world-rank registration must independently bind the same verified unit, and
remote/migrated ranks must refuse. This amendment is implementable at the
software backend but has not been validated with MPI/QE; current adapter
accepts only explicitly allowlisted tiny C workers.

**A different protocol allowing unknown concurrent foreign MPI is a changed
acceptance claim, not a proof of the original one.** It could be considered
only with explicit user approval, independently justified host-wide resource
headroom/reservation and interference assumptions, finite local rank scope,
failure/data-validity criteria, complete disk/output and outside-control budgets,
and all existing state/readback/initial-wave gates. The present family cap alone
does not provide those inputs. It cannot guarantee the host MemAvailable/free
disk floor against unrelated writers/allocators. No such relaxation is adopted
or encoded here; `hostwide_gate` and legacy native admission still refuse.

**Next indispensable inputs:** (1) authoritative continuing host-wide
inventory/exclusion lease, or a separately justified and explicitly approved
different acceptance protocol; (2) complete native producer/backend/disk budget
and actual common 48-band/state/FFT/readback evidence under admitted scope.
The new cap makes a future pilot better controlled; it does not produce missing
physical input, native numerical agreement, restart qualification or x_c.

Stop this bounded software phase after preservation/commit/push. The new host
fact led to real gate advancement, fault discoveries and repairs; additional
small synthetic cases would not decide the remaining native authorization
and material-input boundary. Original 352-band/16-atom HSE+SOC remains excluded.
