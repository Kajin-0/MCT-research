## Precise quantity and conventions

Specify the stationary nonmagnetic relativistic background, common physical
Gamma frame, electrostatic/lattice reference and intended composition interval.
Extract the leading small-q angular norm of the **full self-consistent**
substitution vertex, including induced Te response, in a theory with a controlled
relation to that background. Report the eliminated heavy-hole weighted density,

    rho_8,IR(epsilon) = c8 epsilon^p + R(epsilon),
    |R(epsilon)| <= r(epsilon) epsilon^p,   0<epsilon<Lambda,

with independent bounds on c8,p,r and an explicit error relating that theory
to the intended physical/eliminated response. rho has units of energy, c8
energy^(1-p). The branch threshold, retarded prescription and host/impurity
alignment must be declared. An angle-averaged coherent derivative or arbitrary
partition of binary Te response cannot substitute for this finite-q object.
epsilon is the positive distance from the threshold on the specified continuum
side; downward heavy holes use t=t0-epsilon, not an assumed upward band.

At bare single-impurity/second-Born level this is obtainable from the
symmetry-resolved composite vertex and dispersion. **That bare result alone
does not identify the exact random-alloy augmented-space measure.** A positive
lower-weight statement must survive the controlled resummation/cluster
remainder for a material no-atom conclusion. If that link cannot be bounded,
report a conditional model result and keep the physical question OPEN.

The present HSE trialD0 is nonstationary and cannot supply the required host.
An isolated endpoint impurity also needs an independently bounded
background/concentration adapter before it constrains the crossing domain.
Do not extrapolate its c8 to an unknown interior x_c by assumption.
Obtaining a qualified stationary reference is a prerequisite to calling this

## Why this changes the decision

- If a true lower certificate has c8-r>=c_min>0 and p<=1 at the intended
  branch crossing, B2 excludes a nonzero target spectral atom there. The
  sharp signed-pole x_c program must then use another explicitly operational
  observable, or establish a different channel/physical regime. More spectral
  points or a better endpoint cubic cannot resolve that failed definition.
- If symmetry plus an independently controlled analytic vertex proves
  suppression giving p>1, that particular obstruction is removed. It is NOT
  proof of an atom, Z>=3/4, FD stationarity, topology or a unique x_c. The
  total S and scalar pole equation still matter. B1's simple threshold requires
  S<=1/3, including remote weight, not just a small low-q coefficient.
- If only a fitted small finite-q value or finite-eta peak is available,
  neither outcome is established. Stop with a quantified unresolved c8 bound;
  do not interpret a numerically small allowed term as an exact selection zero.

This ranks above acquiring N3 or another PBE grid **for choosing a sharp

## Decision precision and expected uncertainty

This input initially needs a sign/exponent certificate, not micro-eV accuracy.
If c8 is estimated by c_hat with error delta_c, a decision needs
c_hat-delta_c-sup r>0; otherwise the unsuppressed-channel decision is unresolved.
For p>1 an upper infrared contribution to S is
c_upper Lambda^(p-1)/(p-1), plus the stated remainder and remote bound. As p
approaches1 this becomes ill conditioned. These formulas set the required
accuracy; the current data provide no numerical physical c8, Lambda or S.

If a later full response establishes both Z>=3/4, B1's worst-case resolution
budget is epsilon_Delta<=4eta/3 PLUS the separately propagated Green/projection/
model errors. A target composition precision delta_x requires the total
epsilon_Delta<=s delta_x with independently bounded s. No measured/Hansen slope
is inserted. This does not promise that a chosen eta or another simulation
reaches the target; intrinsic widths/no atom may make it inapplicable.
