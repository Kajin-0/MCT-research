## Search scope and interpretation

Targeted queries covered random spectral support and periodic patterns,
augmented-space configuration averaging, single-site CPA, HgCdTe cation-s
scattering, directional self-energy/tetrahedral response, alloy endpoint
identifiability, and zero-frequency versus real-frequency topological criteria.
Searches did not locate a primary HgCdTe paper making the package's exact
covariance-quotient/root-budget synthesis. That absence is weak evidence and
does not establish novelty. Broad moment non-uniqueness, Schur reduction,
Dyson/Feshbach identities, rare-pattern support, Hermite interpolation and
Neumann bounds are established mathematics.

The original augmented-space citation title was checked against the literature:
the 1340 paper is *Averaged density of states in disordered systems*; the short
L205 paper is *A new formalism for the study of configuration-averaged properties
of disordered systems*. They are not conflated into a fabricated single paper.

The defensible current scientific contribution is a set of explicitly
conditioned HgCdTe identifiability/falsification tests and reduced-input error
criteria. Potential originality of that synthesis remains **POTENTIAL NOVELTY —
UNVERIFIED**. No priority, breakthrough, material validation or manuscript
claim is made.
