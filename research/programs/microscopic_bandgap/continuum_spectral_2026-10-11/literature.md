# Primary methods, measurability and novelty boundary

Bounded review, 2026-10-11. Primary author manuscripts and publisher abstracts
were inspected with native web tools. No paid access, SciDownl installation,
shell paper download or archive outreach was needed. HTML mathematical parsing
can omit symbols; the proofs in this package are independently supplied, rather
than attributed to an unread source. No empirical HgCdTe coefficients enter.

## Established method A — CPA/SCBA spectral propagators

[Soven (1967)](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.156.809)
introduces a complex energy-dependent coherent potential. Its publisher
abstract is accessible; full-text access is restricted. The previous R07
closure gives its governing matrix site-T self-consistency equations.

In the fixed-hopping binary onsite class those equations are
`g_site=integral_BZ [z-H_hop(k)-Sigma]^(-1)_site`,
`t_i=[I-(U_i-Sigma)g_site]^(-1)(U_i-Sigma)` and
`(1-x)t_H+x t_C=0`, with the causal solution selected. The resulting Gamma
candidate is `[z-H_hop(0)-Sigma]^(-1)` projected in the declared frame.
SCBA instead closes the covariance contraction with its own dressed propagator.
Neither equation determines unknown material operators or proves its residual.
[Hass, Ehrenreich and Velicky (1983)](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.27.1088)
is direct HgCdTe SOC tight-binding CPA precedent, with projected spectral
densities and cation-s damping. Publisher abstract inspected; no new accuracy
or physical-input certificate is inferred from its successful model comparison.

CPA or SCBA can produce candidate g6,g8 in a continuum. Their causal branch
does not by itself bound their difference from the physical alloy, omitted
clusters, off-diagonal/directional disorder or many-body response. Applying
S5/S6 to such a model yields a model observable; it becomes a physical bound
only after the model residual is independently certified. The old threshold
cluster indicator and differing CPA edge/peak coefficients remain negative
results, unchanged. Our formulation is not a new effective-medium closure;
it states which output and error information such a closure must supply.

## Established method B — smoothed spectral measures/functional calculus

[Colbrook, Horning and Townsend, Computing spectral measures of self-adjoint
operators](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.01766v2) develops resolvent-based smoothed
spectral measures and pointwise/Lp convergence under local regularity, with
higher-order rational kernels. The accessible author paper's sections 2, 4
and 5 identify the spectral theorem, Poisson-resolvent relation and higher-order
smoothing framework. This directly precedes the present continuum strategy.

Our fixed-eta bounds avoid pointwise density recovery and use positivity for
bounded functionals and conditional centroids. They require no material density
regularity but retain a physical-input budget. A higher-order signed smoothing
kernel cannot automatically use our positivity/conditional-centroid proof;
its kernel variation and sign must be tracked separately. Positive instrumental
or wavepacket responses can replace Poisson smoothing with their independently
known kernel bounds. That is a different declared measurement convention,
not an inference that eta equals experimental linewidth.

No novelty is claimed for spectral positivity, Poisson convolution, resolvent
identities, bounded functional calculus, ratio perturbation or argmax bounds.
The possible R07 contribution is their explicit joint physical-frame/window/
tail/root gate and the reduction to four window integrals. Historical originality
remains **POTENTIAL NOVELTY — UNVERIFIED**; this search is not exhaustive.

## Physical probe and topology restrictions

[Damascelli (2003), Probing the Low-Energy Electronic Structure of Complex
Systems by ARPES](https://arxiv.org/html/cond-mat/0307085), particularly its
spectral-function and matrix-element/finite-resolution sections, supplies
primary author-review context for occupation, matrix elements and response.
An ARPES intensity peak is not a universally normalized sector spectral peak.
The earlier [Stage-0 source matrix](../evidence_acquisition_2026-10-11/stage0_availability_matrix.md)
retains the unavailable raw magneto-optical/assay package. Optical transitions
need current vertices and filling information, not only two one-particle spectra.

[Orlita et al., author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.0969) supplies the
inverted/critical heavy-hole physical context already audited in Stage 0. Its
primary description does not provide the physical projector/error contractions
needed here. No new material support theorem is deduced from that description.

[Qi, Wu and Zhang (2006)](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.74.085308)
and their [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0505308) establish
the two-dimensional topological model context used by information witness N7.
Its degree computation is derived in the package; it is not an HgCdTe model.
[Wang and Zhang (2012)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.1028) concerns interacting
insulators and zero-frequency topological invariants. Those conditions cannot
be imported into a continuum averaged-Gamma peak/centroid crossing. Global
momentum/real-space and spectral/mobility assumptions remain indispensable.

## Search scope

Queries covered smoothed resolvents/spectral measures, CPA's primary origin and
HgCdTe spectral-density precedent, ARPES spectral functions/response, and the
primary two-band topology information example. Full primary HTML was available
for the smoothing and ARPES manuscripts; old CPA claims are limited to publisher
abstracts and the independently derived repository equations. No publication
was found establishing this package as a predictive physical HgCdTe law.
Search absence is not evidence of priority. The finite sprint stops after
three proof/falsification cycles, without escalating a paper search to data
acquisition or refining a rejected microscopic Hamiltonian.
